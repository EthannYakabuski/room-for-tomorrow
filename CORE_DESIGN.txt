ROOM FOR TOMORROW - CORE DESIGN

The player arranges a small furnished home, preserving an entrance and usable
walking space. No timer, lives, paid currency, energy or mandatory advertisements.
Touch a furnishing card; hold over the floor for a footprint ghost; release on a
cell to place. Tap a placed piece to select and move it. Rotate before placing.
Lift returns a piece to the tray. Undo and reset are unlimited and reset itself
can be undone. Paths toggles reachable-floor dots and usable-front indicators.
Plan tip is always free and explains the most relevant unmet rule.

Rules are modelled on integer grid cells, independent of sprite pixels:
- Rotating a rectangular footprint swaps its width and height.
- Furniture must stay inside the floor, avoid all other furniture and structural
  columns, and leave both the entrance and its inside welcome cell clear.
- A breadth-first search from the entrance finds reachable four-neighbour floor.
  At least one cell on each furnishing's facing front must be reachable.
- A required writing desk must overlap a daylight window cell on the back row.
- A floor lamp must be within Manhattan distance two of a chair, sofa, bed or
  dining chair. Distance is measured between the occupied footprints.
- Optional wishes put a fern beside a window, a shelf against a back wall, or a
  cabinet within two steps of a bed. They never prevent ordinary completion.

Content is sixty individually specified briefs, not sixty random seeds. Each has
a name, chapter, dimensions, furnishing list, authored structural cells, windows,
door and optional wish. Commission1-10 teach simple rectangular rooms and the
furnishing types. Sunny Studios narrows daylight positions and varies door
locations. Shared Corners adds interior columns and short dividing walls. Old
Town includes narrow5x8 and wide8x5 rooms and offsets around beams. Garden Rooms
adds grouped courtyard obstructions. Tomorrow's Homes combines furniture needs,
narrow windows and longer stepped structural shapes. It remains a gentle spatial
puzzle with many solutions, not a unique-answer packing challenge.

game/commissions.json stores every concrete definition and a complete recorded
arrangement satisfying mandatory and optional rules. tests/content_authoring.py
contains the explicit briefs and a deterministic arrangement search used during
authoring. The runtime never invokes Python or searches for a solution. Godot's
validator independently verifies each recorded arrangement and finds a second
accepted arrangement for each commission in the model suite. Human testing must
still assess pacing, challenge, readability and whether the sixty commissions
feel sufficiently different; solvability does not establish enjoyable difficulty.

Two separate tutorial rooms introduce placing a chair/light, then desk daylight.
Eight completed commissions open the next chapter. First clear gives35XP and
45/55/65/75/85/95coins by chapter; first optional detail adds10XP/15coins. Replays
remain free; repeating claims cannot duplicate rewards. Planner level is
1+floor(sqrt(XP/40)). Three additional floor palettes cost120/240/360coins and
never change footprints or puzzle rules. Free decoration offers one of each of
the twelve furniture types in an8x8 room and persists separately.

A deterministic UTC daily commission changes each day and offers80coins/30XP
once per day, even when its normal room has already been completed. Two small
quests reward six arrangement actions and one new commission. A recorded highest
day prevents clock rewinds from creating earlier claims; local offline software
cannot prevent deliberate future-clock changes or save editing. Progress,
optional details, cosmetics, tutorial completion, drafts and decoration persist
locally in rooms-v1.json using a temporary file and atomic rename. Saves have a
version, constrained numeric fields and tolerant missing-field defaults.

SharedServices handles native banners, interstitial cadence and Play Games.
The game calls round_finished once for a newly rewarded commission/daily result.
It requests a precise placement hint only when can_reward() is true and reveals
it only after the true earned callback. Tokens make callbacks single-use and
room checks reject a late callback for a different room. Free tips/undo/reset and
all progression remain available when ads are unavailable. Web does not simulate
earned events. Menu layout reserves the actual shared banner height and safe
inset when the optional menu_bottom_reserve API is available. Home content can
scroll inside that safe area without rebuilding the active puzzle.

Sprites are the actual Kenney Furniture Kit Isometric directional PNGs, four
directions per furnishing. Their trimmed frames use one projection scale,
footprint-centred placement and a floor-front bottom anchor; drawing is sorted
by grid depth. Geometry and placement ghosts share the same grid projection.
Furniture/source filenames are mapped in game/furniture.json. Third-party
notices accompany the assets. Original24-second quiet pad is generated from
original sine chords; Kenney interface click is CC0. Audio can be muted.
